You did everything right.
So why does it feel off?

— a question I know from the inside

There's a specific kind of lost that only high-achievers know. The degree, the offer, the title — every box checked. And underneath it, a quiet question that doesn't go away: is this what I actually want, or just what I was supposed to want?

I've spent the better part of a decade living inside that question — through a career I was praised for, a pivot I couldn't explain to anyone at the time, and the slow, unglamorous work of separating what's truly mine from what I'd absorbed from family, culture, and everyone's expectations.

Now I'm turning what I learned into something you can use.

I'm building a guided reflection experience for people navigating a career pivot or a quieter identity shift — a structured way to tell the difference between the expectations you've internalized and the direction that's actually yours. Not advice. Not a personality test. A place to hear yourself think, with the right questions in the right order.

That's all I'm saying publicly for now. The rest, you'll have to try.
