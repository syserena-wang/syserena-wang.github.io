About

Curious by nature,
intentional by practice

I'm an MIT Sloan MBA candidate passionate about bridging business and technology. Before Sloan, I spent seven years leading digital transformation and operational optimization at Dell Technologies and co-founded GoodDeals, a sustainability startup tackling retail food waste.

I aspire to translate user needs into AI-enabled solutions that create measurable social impact. Whether it's driving supply chain optimization with process mining, shipping a hackathon-winning prototype, or building a product roadmap from scratch — I bring both rigor and heart.

I'm also a registered yoga teacher who cares deeply about wellbeing, connection, and creating space for ideas to grow. I believe the best teams are ones where people feel safe being honest and creative at the same time.
