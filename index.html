About

Curious by nature,
intentional by practice

I'm an MIT Sloan MBA candidate building at the intersection of technology, behavior, and human wellbeing. Before Sloan, I spent seven years leading digital transformation and operational optimization at Dell Technologies and co-founded GoodDeals, a sustainability startup tackling retail food waste.

Today I'm a solo founder building an AI product that helps high-achievers reclaim their attention from social media — through reflection and accountability, not another dopamine loop. It grew out of a conviction I've been circling for years: that the platforms shaping modern life were engineered to extract attention, and that the most necessary thing to build right now is something for humans, not infrastructure.

I'm also a registered yoga teacher who cares deeply about wellbeing, connection, and creating space for ideas to grow. I bring both rigor and heart — and I believe the best teams are ones where people feel safe being honest and creative at the same time.
