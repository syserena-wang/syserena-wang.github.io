TIPS · 小提醒
Cusco is at 3,400m — your first night is critical for altitude acclimatization庫斯科海拔 3,400 公尺，第一晚對於高原適應非常關鍵
No intense exercise, no alcohol, no hot showers on the first night第一晚不要劇烈運動、不要喝酒、不要洗熱水澡
Eat light — high altitude affects digestion飲食清淡，高海拔會影響消化
If you have Diamox, start taking it before the flight. You can also buy Sorojchi Pills (local altitude sickness medicine) at any pharmacy in Cusco city center — the airport itself doesn't have much如果有 Diamox，在搭機前開始服用。也可以在庫斯科市區的藥局買Sorojchi Pills（當地高山症藥物），機場本身沒什麼店
Beware of taxi touts at Cusco airport — local taxis swarm arriving tourists and may overcharge. Use Uber instead for a safer, fairer-priced ride to your accommodation注意庫斯科機場的計程車攬客，當地計程車會圍上剛到的觀光客，可能漫天要價。搭 Uber 到住處更安全、價格更合理
Cusco is in the mountains — weather can change quickly and drastically. Build buffer time into your mountain itinerary in case flights are delayed or cancelled. Our flight couldn't land and we lost half a day庫斯科在山區，天氣可以瞬間劇烈變化。山區行程盡量多留緩衝時間，以防航班延誤或取消。我們的航班無法降落，整整耽誤了半天
LATAM Airlines has far more Lima↔Cusco flights than other carriers. If anything goes wrong, it's much easier to reschedule with LATAM. Worth considering even if slightly more expensiveLATAM Airlines 的利馬↔庫斯科航班比其他航空公司多很多。如果發生任何狀況，改簽 LATAM 的班機容易得多。即使稍貴一些也值得考慮

🏔️ Altitude Sickness Prevention · 高山症預防
Start Diamox the day before arriving in Cusco. Locally, buy Sorojchi Pills at any pharmacy到庫斯科前一天開始服用 Diamox。當地可在任何藥局買到 Sorojchi Pills
Drink at least 2–3L of water daily. Avoid alcohol, especially the first two days每天至少喝 2–3 公升的水。避免喝酒，特別是前兩天

🗓️ When to Visit · 旅遊季節
The Andes (Cusco, Machu Picchu, the treks) have two seasons: dry season May–October (sunny days, clear mountain views, freezing nights) and wet season November–March (afternoon downpours, heaviest rain in January–February)安地斯山區（庫斯科、馬丘比丘、健行路線）分兩季：乾季 5–10 月（白天晴朗、山景清晰、夜晚接近冰點）和雨季 11–3 月（午後暴雨，1–2 月雨最大）
Peak season is June–August — the best weather, but the biggest crowds and highest prices. Book Machu Picchu circuit tickets and trains 2–3+ months ahead; Circuit 2A and Huayna Picchu sell out first. Cusco is also packed around Inti Raymi (the Sun Festival, June 24)旺季是 6–8 月，天氣最好，但人最多、價格最高。馬丘比丘門票和火車要提前 2–3 個月以上預訂，2A 路線和 Huayna Picchu 最先售完。太陽祭 Inti Raymi（6 月 24 日）前後庫斯科也擠滿人
Shoulder months (April–May, September–October) are the sweet spot — decent weather, greener valleys, fewer crowds, and much easier bookings4–5 月和 9–10 月是甜蜜點：天氣不錯、山谷更綠、人潮較少、訂票容易得多
We went in March (tail end of the rainy season): tickets were easy (we bought Circuit 2A in person the day before!) and trails were quiet — but expect mud, pack rain gear, and build in buffer for weather disruptions. Our Cusco flight couldn't land because of sudden weather. Rainbow Mountain can even get snow, which hides the colored stripes我們是3 月（雨季尾聲）去的：門票好買（2A 我們前一天現場才買到！）、步道人少，但要有面對泥濘的準備、帶好雨具，並為天氣狀況多留緩衝時間。我們的庫斯科航班就因突然變天無法降落。彩虹山甚至可能下雪，把彩色條紋蓋住
The classic Inca Trail closes every February for maintenance — Machu Picchu itself stays open year-round經典印加古道每年 2 月關閉維護，馬丘比丘本身則全年開放
Lima runs on the opposite schedule: grey, cool, and overcast May–November (the coastal garúa mist), sunny and warm December–April. Actual rain is rare there in any month利馬的季節剛好相反：5–11 月灰暗涼爽多雲（沿海霧氣 garúa），12–4 月晴朗溫暖。利馬其實整年都很少真正下雨
Whenever you go, pack for four seasons in the mountains — intense midday sun, and day-night temperature swings of ~20°C are normal at altitude不管哪個月去，山區都要照四季打包：中午陽光強烈，高海拔日夜溫差約 20°C 很正常

🔒 Safety · 安全
Safe areas for tourists in Lima: Miraflores (safest), Barranco (artsy, safe by day), San Isidro (business district). Avoid unfamiliar neighborhoods at night利馬觀光客安全區域：Miraflores（最安全）、Barranco（文藝區，白天安全）、San Isidro（商業區）。夜晚避免前往不熟悉的區域
Use Uber for city transport — Uber Comfort is recommended over regular Uber for a safer, more comfortable ride. Avoid street taxis in Lima市區交通搭Uber，建議選 Uber Comfort，比普通 Uber 更安全舒適。在利馬避免搭路邊計程車
Don't use your phone while walking — pickpockets target crowded areas (markets, bus stations)走路時不要滑手機，扒手專門在人多的地方（市場、公車站）下手

🍲 Common Peruvian Dishes · 常見秘魯菜餚
Ceviche — Fresh raw fish marinated in lime juice with red onion, chili, and cilantro. Peru's national dish — best at lunch when fish is freshest新鮮生魚用檸檬汁醃製，搭配紅洋蔥、辣椒和香菜。秘魯國菜，午餐吃最新鮮
Causa — Layered cold potato terrine with avocado and chicken or tuna filling. Bright yellow from ají amarillo chili冷馬鈴薯千層，夾酪梨和雞肉或鮪魚。因 ají amarillo 黃辣椒而呈亮黃色
Lomo Saltado — Stir-fried beef strips with onions, tomatoes, soy sauce, served over fries and rice. Chinese-Peruvian fusion (Chifa) origins醬油炒牛肉條配洋蔥、番茄，搭配薯條和白飯。源自中秘融合料理（Chifa）
Ají de Gallina — Shredded chicken in a creamy, mildly spicy yellow chili sauce with walnuts, served over rice奶油黃辣椒核桃醬燉雞絲，搭配白飯。微辣，口感濃郁
Anticuchos — Grilled beef heart skewers — a beloved Peruvian street food. Tender and flavorful, served with potatoes and corn烤牛心串，深受喜愛的秘魯街頭小吃。口感軟嫩鮮美，搭配馬鈴薯和玉米
Rocoto Relleno — Stuffed spicy pepper filled with seasoned ground meat, topped with melted cheese. A Cusco/Arequipa specialty辣椒鑲肉，填入調味絞肉，上面覆蓋融化起司。庫斯科／阿雷基帕特色菜
Cuy — Roasted guinea pig — Cusco's most iconic dish. Crispy skin, similar to suckling pig. An adventurous must-try烤天竺鼠，庫斯科最具代表性的菜餚。外皮酥脆，口感類似烤乳豬。冒險必嚐
Pollo a la Brasa — Peruvian rotisserie chicken marinated in spices. Juicy, affordable, and everywhere — Peru's everyday comfort food秘魯香料烤雞。多汁、平價、到處都有，秘魯的日常美食
Alpaca Steak — Lean, tender meat similar to beef but lower in fat. Common in Cusco restaurants — try it grilled瘦嫩肉質類似牛肉但脂肪更低。庫斯科餐廳常見，推薦炭烤
Chicha Morada — Sweet purple corn drink boiled with pineapple, cinnamon, and cloves. Non-alcoholic and refreshing紫玉米甜飲，加鳳梨、肉桂、丁香煮成。無酒精，清爽好喝
Pisco Sour — Peru's national cocktail — pisco, lime juice, egg white, syrup, and bitters. Frothy and potent秘魯國民調酒，Pisco 白蘭地、檸檬汁、蛋白、糖漿和苦精。泡沫綿密，後勁強
Mate de Coca (Coca Tea) — Tea brewed from coca leaves — widely consumed at altitude to ease headaches, nausea, and fatigue. Legal in Peru, served at hotels and restaurants everywhere in Cusco古柯葉泡的茶，在高海拔地區廣泛飲用，可緩解頭痛、噁心和疲勞。在秘魯合法，庫斯科的飯店和餐廳到處都有
Muña Tea — Andean mint tea made from a wild herb native to the highlands. Helps with digestion and altitude discomfort. Refreshing, slightly minty flavor安地斯薄荷茶，用高地原生野生香草沖泡。有助消化和緩解高海拔不適。口感清爽，帶有淡淡薄荷味
